Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Checkers Master Web & Mobile Edition

1. Overview

Welcome to Checkers Master. We respect your privacy and provide this policy to explain how our game operates with respect to player privacy, browser data, and device access.

Privacy Promise: Checkers Master does not require sign-in, does not collect personal contact information, and processes all board logic and AI matches locally on your device.

2. Device & Local Storage Usage

The game stores minimal gameplay preferences inside your browser's localStorage:

  • Preferences: Board theme selection (Mahogany, Cyber Neon, Marble), sound effects volume, and music toggle state.
  • Game Rules: Mandatory jumps toggle and selected AI difficulty.
  • Statistics: Win/loss tally and move history if saved locally.

No telemetry or personal profiles are transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Fonts

We use Google Fonts to load typography cleanly. Google may collect standard web logs (IP address, user agent) to deliver web fonts reliably. We do not use third-party user-tracking pixels or cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Children's Privacy

Checkers Master is family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect any personal information from children under 13.

5. Managing Your Data

You can reset all stored preferences, stats, and audio settings at any time simply by clearing your browser cache and local storage for this website.

6. Inquiries

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, please contact us through the game's open-source repository or project maintainer.
