How to Play

Master the board with classic Checkers rules

1

Dark Squares & Diagonals

Checkers is played exclusively on the 32 dark squares of an 8×8 board. Red plays first.

2

Forward Movement Only

Standard pieces move diagonally 1 step forward to an unoccupied adjacent dark square (when no jumps are available).

Jumping & Removing Pieces

If an opponent piece is diagonally in front of you and the square directly behind it is empty, you jump over it to capture and remove it.

⚠️

Mandatory Capture (Forced Jump)

In official Checkers rules, captures are mandatory. If a jump is available anywhere on the board for your pieces, you MUST make a jump. Regular 1-step moves are disabled until all available captures are completed.

🔒 Forced Jump Rule: If you or your opponent can capture a piece, no other non-jumping piece can be moved instead. If multiple pieces can jump, you can choose which jump to make!
⚙️ Main Menu Option: Turn off Mandatory Jumps in the Main Menu if you prefer casual free play where captures and multi-jumps are optional!
⚡

Multi-Jump Chains

If your piece lands on a square that allows another legal jump, you keep jumping in the same turn to capture multiple pieces!

👑

Crown Promotion

When your piece reaches the opponent's furthest back row, it is crowned a King!

✨

Full 360° Diagonal Power

Kings can move and jump both forwards AND backwards diagonally, giving you immense tactical control over the board.

How to Win

You achieve victory when:

  • You capture all 12 of your opponent's pieces.
  • You trap your opponent so they have no legal moves remaining.
🧠

Grandmaster Strategies

  • Protect your back row: Don't move your last row pieces too early so the enemy cannot easily make Kings.
  • Control the center: Pieces in the center have 2 diagonal paths, while edge pieces only have 1.
  • Set up multi-jumps: Bait your opponent by sacrificing 1 piece to capture 2 or 3 of theirs in return!
